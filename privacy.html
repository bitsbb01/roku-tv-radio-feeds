JGJG TV & Radio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the JGJG TV & Radio Roku app handles information. The app does not require a JGJG account.

Information stored on your Roku

Your channel favorites and app settings are stored locally in the Roku app registry. If you link Plex, the app receives a Plex authentication token and stores it in the Roku app registry so it can connect to Plex when you browse your libraries. The app does not send this information to a JGJG-operated server.

Connections to other services

The app requests playlists, programme-guide data, logos, and streams from the third-party services whose links appear in the app. If you choose Plex, the app also communicates with Plex and the Plex servers you select. Those services may receive your device's network address and request information, and may process it under their own privacy policies. Your use of Plex is also subject to Plex's terms and privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

The current app release does not include a JGJG account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. Roku and third-party service providers may separately collect technical or usage information under their own policies.

Managing stored information

You can stop using Plex by revoking the app's access in your Plex account settings. Favorites and settings are held on the Roku device. Removing the app or clearing its stored data through Roku device settings may remove that local information.

Children

The app is a general-audience media app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not ask users to provide personal details to JGJG.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data handling change. The latest version will be posted on this page. For questions about this policy, visit jgjgbooks.com.
